PROPOSAL: Google Login Integration
Feature Name: Google Login
Marcus Rivera proposed introducing Google Login as an alternative authentication method.

in the onboarding process and eliminating the need for password management for those users.

Reason for Proposal:

- Password reset support tickets are high-cost to manage
