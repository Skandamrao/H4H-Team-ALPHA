Date: October 15, 2024
Meeting: Authentication Module Review
Participants: Sarah Chen, Marcus Rivera, Priya Patel, Daniel Kim (UX Designer)

AGENDA ITEM 1: Post-Launch Review of Google Authentication

The Google OAuth Integration launched on October 12 and has been live for 3 days.
Early metrics show a 28% sign-in rate via Google in the first 48 hours.

User feedback is positive. Support tickets for login issues have already decreased.

AGENDA ITEM 2: Password Authentication Module — Deprecation Decision

The team revisited the earlier discussion about deprecating the password authentication 
module (discussed but NOT decided on October 5).

PROPOSAL: Deprecate Password Authentication Module
Feature Name: Password Authentication
Marcus Rivera proposed a phased deprecation of the password authentication module 
with a target removal date of Q1 2025.

Arguments in favour:
- Google OAuth is available and stable
- Password management represents ongoing security overhead
- Future: add more OAuth providers (GitHub, Microsoft)

Arguments against:
- Not all users have Google accounts
- Enterprise customers may require password auth
- Accessibility concerns raised by Daniel Kim

STATUS: PROPOSED — team voted to take this to the wider engineering team for review.
No removal decision has been made.

AGENDA ITEM 3: Rename of Authentication Module

Sarah Chen proposed renaming the module from "Authentication Module" to 
"Identity and Access Module" to better reflect the planned expansion to 
cover authorization (RBAC) in addition to authentication.

Decision: APPROVED — the module will be renamed.
Effective date: November 1, 2024.
